WHO I AM

I'm a SaaS founder and emerging AI Engineer based in Ontario, Canada. I built Detours — a full-stack transport management platform — from scratch using React, TypeScript, and Supabase.

What started as a tool for 3 trucks is now a production-grade multi-tenant SaaS handling dispatch, live GPS tracking, POD submissions, driver settlements, fleet management and automated invoicing.

I'm now going deeper — learning Python, ML, deep learning, NLP, LLMs, RAG and agents. My goal is to build AI features directly into Detours and work as an AI Engineer.
